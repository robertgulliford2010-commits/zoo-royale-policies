# ZOO Royale — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 19 October 2026**

This policy explains how information is handled when you interact with ZOO Royale, a Discord-based animal battling and progression game.

**Privacy contact:** quackbenchofficial@outlook.com

## 1. Information involved
Depending on the feature used, the game may process:
- Discord user identifiers, display names, server and channel identifiers, and other Discord interaction metadata.
- Player profiles, animal ownership, experience, levels, virtual currency and item balances, achievements, rewards, and similar progression records.
- Battle and event participation, results, rankings, and game command interactions.
- Operational records such as errors or logs needed to diagnose problems, investigate misuse, and keep the game working.

Ordinary gameplay does not require you to submit your real name, home address, or payment card details directly to ZOO Royale.

## 2. Purposes
These records are used to identify game accounts, maintain progress, run commands and events, calculate rewards and leaderboards, resolve support issues, maintain security, and prevent misuse.

## 3. Legal bases
Where UK or European data-protection law applies, processing may be based on legitimate interests in providing and protecting the game, fulfilling the service you request, compliance with legal obligations, or another applicable lawful basis depending on the activity. You can ask about the basis for a particular use at the address below.

## 4. Visibility and service providers
Game-generated information, such as player display names, standings, and battle results, may appear publicly to participants in relevant Discord servers or channels. Discord itself processes information under its own policies. Hosting, storage, and technical infrastructure providers may process information as needed to operate ZOO Royale. We do not offer players' personal information for sale.

## 5. Retention and security
Game data is kept while needed to support player accounts and the service, resolve disputes and bugs, protect against misuse, or meet applicable legal requirements. Retention periods vary by record type, and backups may persist for a limited period after deletion from active systems. We use reasonable security precautions, but no internet service is completely secure.

## 6. Your rights and deletion requests
Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, or restrict the use of personal information, to object to certain uses, or to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. You can request help or deletion of data associated with your Discord account by emailing **quackbenchofficial@outlook.com**. We may need to verify that you control the relevant Discord account before acting. Some records may need to be retained where law or legitimate security needs require it.

## 7. Age requirements
The game is intended for people allowed to use Discord under Discord's minimum-age rules and applicable law. It is not intended to solicit additional personal information from children.

## 8. Changes
We may revise this policy if the service or data practices change. We will update the effective date and communicate material changes where reasonably practicable.

## 9. Contact
For privacy questions or requests: **quackbenchofficial@outlook.com**.
