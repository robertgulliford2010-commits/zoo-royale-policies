# ZOO Royale — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 19 October 2026**

This policy explains how information is handled when you use ZOO Royale, a Discord-based game, including any optional purchases offered through Discord.

**Privacy contact:** quackbenchofficial@outlook.com

## 1. Data handled by the game
Depending on the features used, ZOO Royale may process:
- Discord user IDs, display names, server/channel IDs, and interaction metadata needed to operate the game.
- Animal ownership and species unlocks, progress, levels, virtual currency, achievements, rewards, battles, events, and leaderboard information.
- Game commands, related activity and technical error/security logs.
- **If premium features are offered:** Discord SKU and entitlement identifiers, the relevant Discord user ID, entitlement status, and records needed to grant, restore, or revoke premium access and investigate purchase-support issues.

Ordinary play and Discord-based purchases do **not** require you to send your payment card details directly to ZOO Royale. Discord handles checkout and billing through its systems; payment information you provide to Discord is subject to Discord's own privacy terms. ZOO Royale may receive the limited purchase/entitlement status information made available to the app, not full card numbers or security codes.

## 2. Why data is used
We process the relevant information to identify game accounts, preserve progress, operate battles/events, show standings, handle commands, verify ownership of optional purchases, unlock paid species, restore access after outages, respond to support and privacy enquiries, and prevent cheating, fraud, or technical misuse.

## 3. Legal bases
Where UK or European data-protection law applies, relevant legal bases may include providing a service requested by a user, legitimate interests in operating and securing the game, and compliance with legal obligations, as appropriate to each use. Contact us to ask about a specific processing activity.

## 4. Visibility, Discord, and providers
Some gameplay information (such as Discord display names, results, and rankings) may be visible in participating Discord servers. Discord separately handles its platform services, payments, and billing under its own policies. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process data as necessary to run ZOO Royale. ZOO Royale does not offer player personal information for sale.

## 5. Retention and security
Game progression and entitlement-related records may be retained while needed to maintain accounts and purchased access, address support and fraud issues, protect the service, or comply with applicable law. Technical logs and backups may have different retention periods, and deleted information may remain temporarily in backups. Reasonable security measures are used, but no service is risk-free.

## 6. Your rights and deletion requests
Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or objection, and to complain to an applicable data-protection authority. Email **quackbenchofficial@outlook.com** with the relevant Discord user ID and your request; we may verify account control before acting. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or prevent abuse. Deleting a ZOO Royale player profile may affect in-game progress or the ability to deliver an unlock; a valid Discord purchase entitlement is managed separately by Discord and does not itself guarantee restored deleted game progress.

For payment-method changes, billing information, or Discord checkout disputes, use Discord's official support and account settings. Never email us your full card number or security code.

## 7. Age requirements
ZOO Royale is intended for people permitted to use Discord under its minimum-age rules and applicable law.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy if features, purchase integrations, or data practices change. We will update the date and announce material changes where reasonably practicable.
